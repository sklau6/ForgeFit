Frequently asked questions

Does ForgeFit need an internet connection?

No. ForgeFit is fully functional offline. Programs, exercises, meals and breathing drills are built into the app.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I change the language or theme?

Open Settings (gear icon, top right) and pick your language and theme. The change applies instantly and you stay on the same screen. 11 languages are supported, including Traditional Chinese.

How do I reset my progress?

Delete the app and reinstall it. All data is local, so this clears everything.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or later; Android phones and tablets on Android 8.0 (API 26) or later.

I found a bug. What should I send you?

Email us your device model, OS version, app version, and what you did just before the problem. A screenshot helps a lot.
