Privacy Policy — ForgeFit

Last updated: 23 September 2026

ForgeFit (“the app”) is developed by Shu Keung LAU (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

ForgeFit does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we store, and where

All data you create in ForgeFit — readiness check-ins, workout logs, nutrition and water entries, habit streaks, recovery and sleep notes, and your settings (language, theme) — is stored locally on your device.

This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is not sent to us or to any third party,
  • is not used for advertising or analytics,
  • is deleted when you delete the app.

What we do not collect

  • name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • account credentials (the app has no account or login)
  • location data
  • health or fitness data from Apple Health / HealthKit / Google Fit
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or analytics events
  • crash reports or diagnostics sent to us
  • photos, contacts, or any other personal content

Network access

ForgeFit is fully functional offline and does not send your data anywhere.

Children

ForgeFit does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on our side. Delete the app to remove all app data from your device.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or social network SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.
