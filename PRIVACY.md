# Privacy Policy — ForgeFit

**Last updated: 23 September 2026**

ForgeFit ("the app") is developed by Shu Keung LAU ("we", "us"). This policy explains
what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Summary

**ForgeFit does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** Everything you enter
stays on your device.

## What we store, and where

All data you create in ForgeFit — your readiness check-ins, workout logs, nutrition and
water entries, habit streaks, recovery and sleep notes, and your settings (language,
theme) — is stored **locally on your device** using Apple's on-device storage
(UserDefaults / SwiftData) and Android's on-device storage.

This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is not sent to us or to any third party,
- is not used for advertising or analytics,
- is deleted when you delete the app.

## What we do not collect

We do **not** collect, request, or process:

- name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- account credentials (the app has no account or login)
- location data
- health or fitness data from Apple Health / HealthKit / Google Fit
- device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or analytics events
- crash reports or diagnostics sent to us
- photos, contacts, or any other personal content

## Network access

The app is fully functional offline. It does not require a network connection and does
not send your data anywhere. If a build is configured with an optional content-update
endpoint, it requests only public content files and transmits none of your personal data.

## Children

ForgeFit does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children
under 13.

## Your rights

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or
delete on our side. You can remove all app data at any time by deleting the app from
your device.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social
network SDKs.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new
