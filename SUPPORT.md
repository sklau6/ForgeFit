## Frequently asked questions

**Does ForgeFit need an internet connection?**
No. ForgeFit is fully functional offline. Everything — programs, exercises, meals,
breathing drills — is built into the app.

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start training.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device only. Nothing is uploaded. See the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

**How do I change the language or theme?**
Open Settings (the gear icon, top right) and pick your language and theme. The app keeps
you on the same screen and applies the change instantly. 11 languages are supported,
including Traditional Chinese.

**How do I reset my progress?**
Delete the app and reinstall it. All data is local, so this clears everything.

**Which devices are supported?**
iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or later, and Android phones and tablets on Android 8.0
(API 26) or later.

**I found a bug. What should I send you?**
Email us with your device model, OS version, app version, and what you did just before
the problem. A screenshot helps a lot.
